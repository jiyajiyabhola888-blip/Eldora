Our Story

Why We Started CareNest

CareNest was created with one simple mission — to ensure that every elderly person receives trusted, respectful, and compassionate care. Many families struggle to find verified caregivers for short-term support, hospital visits, social events, or everyday companionship. We wanted to make this process simple, transparent, and reliable.

Our platform connects families with verified care companions while providing real-time updates, secure bookings, live attendance, transparent profiles, and complete peace of mind.

Our Mission

To make elder care accessible, trustworthy, and affordable by connecting families with verified care companions for home care, hospital visits, travel assistance, events, and daily support.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted elder care platform where every senior citizen receives dignity, safety, companionship, and professional support whenever needed.

Our Values

Compassion, Trust, Transparency, Respect, Safety, Innovation, and Human Connection are the values that guide every service offered by CareNest.
