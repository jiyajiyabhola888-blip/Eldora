Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a caretaker?

Visit the Services page, choose a service, fill the booking form and confirm your booking.

Are CareNest caretakers verified?

Yes. Every caretaker goes through ID verification and background checks before joining.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. You can cancel your booking anytime from the My Bookings page.

Do you provide emergency care?

Yes. Emergency care services are available 24×7 in supported locations.

Which cities are supported?

CareNest supports multiple cities and will continue expanding globally.
